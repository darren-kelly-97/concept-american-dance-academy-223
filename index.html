How the season runs.

  1. September

    The first class of the 2026-27 season is Saturday, September 12.

  2. Holiday show

    An optional show for dancers who want an extra performance each year.

  3. Spring recital

    The season closes with a dance recital in spring.

  4. Summer

    Dance camps and intensives keep dancers moving between seasons.

Two studios built for dancers.

Both dance rooms have hardwood sprung floors and high ceilings. Our floating floors are built with a special cushion to help prevent injury and help dancers find height in jumps and leaps.

Watch from the waiting room
A large waiting room with a 36" viewing monitor for each dance room.
A lounge for older students
A space in the back to relax, do homework and get ready for class.
Easy drop-off and pick-up
Lantana Square on Route 7 has ample parking and plenty of food and shopping nearby.

Every ADA teacher has spent 5 years or more at the studio, with backgrounds in dance, music, education and child development.

ADA Dance Company

Ages 6 and up

For serious, technical dancers. Company members train many hours each week, study year-round with guest teachers and choreographers, and compete at the highest level.

Community performances

Ages 4 and up

For dancers who want to perform more without the Company schedule: parades, local sports games, the holiday show and visits to a nursing home.
